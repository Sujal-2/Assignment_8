# Stakeholder Analysis

| Stakeholder | Interest / problem | Influence | Engagement |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Business owner / sponsor | Accurate margin, stock value, growth, continuity | High | Sprint review and monthly KPI review |
| Inventory manager | Correct batches, expiry, counts, alerts | High | Product owner proxy; weekly backlog refinement |
| Sales officer | Fast availability check and invoice creation | Medium | Prototype/UAT in every release |
| Purchasing officer | Supplier lead times, purchase receiving, reorders | Medium | Workflow review and exception testing |
| Warehouse staff | Simple receiving, picking, stock count | Medium | Observation and usability sessions |
| Finance / auditor | Traceable invoices, valuation, audit export | High | Control review before release |
| Hospital/clinic/pharmacy customer | Correct order, price, delivery and invoice | Medium | Feedback survey and support analysis |
| System administrator | Security, backups, user access, monitoring | High | Architecture and operational readiness review |
| Regulators / tax authority | Retention, traceability, local compliance | High | Compliance checklist and change monitoring |
| Suppliers | Accurate purchase orders and dispute evidence | Low | Periodic data-quality review |

No patient or clinical data is required. If future scope introduces such data, a separate privacy, consent, retention, and regulatory assessment is mandatory.
